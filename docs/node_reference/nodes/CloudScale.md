
CloudScale Node
===============


CloudScale scales the coordinates of the points in a cloud relative to a specified center point.



![img](../../images/nodes/CloudScale_settings.png)


## Category


Geometry/Cloud
## Inputs

|Name|Type|Description|
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
|input|Cloud|Input cloud.|

## Outputs

|Name|Type|Description|
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
|output|Cloud|Output scaled cloud.|

## Parameters

|Name|Type|Description|
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
|Center|Vec2Float|Center of scaling.|
|Scale|Float|Scale factor.|
