
CloudMerge Node
===============


CloudMerge merges two clouds into a single one.



![img](../../images/nodes/CloudMerge_settings.png)


## Category


Geometry/Cloud
## Inputs

|Name|Type|Description|
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
|cloud1|Cloud|Input cloud.|
|cloud2|Cloud|Input cloud.|

## Outputs

|Name|Type|Description|
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
|cloud|Cloud|Merged cloud.|
